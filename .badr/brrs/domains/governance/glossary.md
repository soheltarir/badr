# Governance glossary

- **Business rule record (BRR)**: A named business policy expressed through observable examples.
- **Architecture decision record (ADR)**: A record of an architecture choice, its context, rationale, and consequences.
- **Approval**: An explicitly declared review decision; it does not authenticate the reviewer.
- **Effective rule**: An approved business rule that has been explicitly activated and has not been withdrawn.
- **Directing decision**: An approved ADR that governs new work.
- **Baseline**: The selected integration branch used to establish durable record history.
- **Replacement**: An explicit complete transfer of authority from predecessors to successors; withdrawal of a successor does not revive predecessors.
- **Evidence**: Retained receipts describing record allocation and governed changes.

These definitions accompany retrospective draft records seeded from the implementation at `11a64e2`.
